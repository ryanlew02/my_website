Inner City

Build your city, one habit at a time.

React Native Expo TypeScript SQL

About

Inner City is a mobile habit-tracking app that turns daily routines into a city-building game. Complete your habits each day to construct buildings and watch your city grow on an isometric grid.

Features

  • Track daily habits with customizable goals and streaks
  • Isometric city that grows as you complete habits
  • Detailed stats with overview, weekly, monthly, and yearly views
  • Calendar heatmaps to visualize consistency
  • Support for both "build" and "quit" habit types
